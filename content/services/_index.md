## Find your profile system

Browse our [casement and sliding series](/products/) and compare the product drawings, wall thicknesses, weights, and bag quantities. Each profile includes a catalogue reference to make it easier to identify the item you need.

## Prepare your requirement

For a product enquiry, include:

- The profile series and product code.
- The catalogue page and entry number, especially where a code has more than one specification.
- Your required quantity in lengths, bags, or metres.
- Your company name, project location, and contact details.

## Confirm availability and pricing

[Contact Kalpana Traders](/contact/) to discuss the selected specifications, current availability, pricing, and supply arrangements for your requirements.
